# The newspaper component

The newspaper component is a whole front page in one tile, meant to fill the grid (`renders/items/_newspaper`, its `newspaper/` partials, `.paper-*` in `render.css`). It takes RSS feeds, a weather source and calendars together. Its layouts are types of paper (`NewspaperStyle`): serious broadsheet, tabloid, punk zine, patriot, wizarding gazette, 90s hacker, classic Mac, classic Windows (3.1), CDE, and custom, whose fonts, arrangement and capitals come from settings (a `:select` setting type lists the choices). A style sets a font for each role (masthead, headline, small headline, text, label, and optionally chrome, which `NewspaperStyle#css` hands `render.css` as `--paper-chrome`) from `NewspaperFont`, an arrangement (broadsheet: three columns; tabloid: the lead across two thirds), and decorations `render.css` draws for `.paper--<style>` (tabloid's black masthead, the zine's cut-out letters from `NewsHelper#ransom_letters` and photocopied photos, the patriot's flag of stars from `NewsHelper#pixel_art`, the wizarding gazette's hollow stretched name, oval photo and stamp, the classic Mac's and Windows' windows on a dotted desktop under a menu bar made from the dateline, CDE's Motif windows on granite over a Front Panel made from the dateline, with a workspace switcher; the `classic_mac`, `classic_windows` and `cde` dashboard themes draw every tile as the same kind of window, its header the title bar, with Windows' title bar controls as the `--win-*` masks in `:root` and CDE's controls, frame, gray and granite as the `--cde-*` ones). Weird type stays on the pixel grid: stretches are whole-number scales set at half size so the fitting still measures real space, column headlines can shift letter by letter by whole pixels (`NewsHelper#shifted_letters`: the wizard's wave, the zine's jumble, the hacker's glitch; a pattern inverts only a headline's first letter, never one inside it), and a side strip of the motto (or its binary) turns a whole quarter. The hacker paper swaps ink and paper for its whole card with `:has`. Only boxes and photos tilt freely. The lead is the newest story with a photo; the next photo story, a big headline from another source, an "Upcoming events" box (the calendars' events under way or starting within `event_hours`, labeled by `NewsHelper#newspaper_event_when`) and briefs with sources taking turns (`NewsFeed.by_turns`) fill the columns. Headlines are never clipped: an inline script in the partial runs once fonts load, stepping each box's first headline down its ladder (`NewspaperStyle#ladder`, `.hl-<cut>-<px>` classes) until it fits, then dropping the events box's latest events, then keeping later stories only while they fit. Stories that fit nowhere then fill the gaps at the foot of the columns at their smallest headline. It also shrinks the name to fit between the ears. The fonts are pixel fonts, each cut set only at a whole multiple of its measured grid; `NewspaperStyle#css` writes a `<style>` in the tile with just the cuts it uses (as data: URIs via `InlineAssets.font`, skipping ones `render.css` already declares) and its size classes, scoped to the tile. They're OFL or CC0 (texts in `app/assets/fonts/licenses`), stored as unmodified woff2 (no subsetting: some reserve their names under the OFL). Parts: weather, dateline, photos, events, bylines, summaries. `_item` gives every card a `card--<kind>` class, which the newspaper uses to drop its border. Aggregator headlines lose the outlet suffix their `<source>` names.
